## Do more
- **Reels.** All 7 reels reached 193–2,735 people; all 3 carousels reached 1–4 (only followers). Reels are what brings non-followers.
- **Short toon reels (~20–35 s).** The two ~60 s engine-3 reels kept viewers only 9–12% of their length (6.0 s / 7.2 s) with view rates 37–38%; the toon reels kept 25–54% (e.g. 16.6 s of 31.6 s, 10.3 s of 19.1 s).
- **Hooks built on a surprise or contradiction in the first line**: "astronauts get taller", "sky is blue although sunlight is white", "brain is 2% of weight but uses a fifth of energy". These 3 have the 3 best average watch times (16.6 s, 10.1 s, 10.3 s).
- **Space angle (tentative, 2 items):** space-taller and brain-energy (the brain-in-space twist) are the two reels with the best watch ratio (>50%).

- **Ending captions with a generic question as the only comment driver** — every item ended with a "👇" question and got 0 comments (10 items).
